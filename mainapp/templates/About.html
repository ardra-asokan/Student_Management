CampusConnect Portal

CampusConnect Portal is a digital system designed to simplify academic management for colleges. It allows administrators, teachers, and students to access essential academic information quickly and securely.

  • Centralized Access: All academic data—attendance, marks, assignments, and profiles—are stored in one unified platform.
  • Role-Based Dashboards: Separate, easy-to-use dashboards for Admins, Teachers, and Students for smoother workflow.
  • Real-Time Updates: Instant notifications for attendance updates, assignment uploads, exam schedules, and published results.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build a smart and user-friendly academic platform that reduces manual workload, eliminates paperwork, and provides seamless access to academic data for students, teachers, and administrators.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a fully digital and connected campus environment where learning, teaching, and management happen effortlessly through a unified portal, enabling better communication, transparency, and academic excellence.

What the Portal Does

The portal provides role-based features for Administrators, Teachers, and Students to ensure smooth and efficient academic management.

Admin Features

  • • Manage student records
  • • Add/update teachers
  • • Create subjects & timetables
  • • Upload results
  • • Manage attendance

Teacher Features

  • • Mark attendance
  • • Upload marks
  • • Give assignments
  • • View student reports

Student Features

  • • View attendance
  • • Check marks & results
  • • Download/upload assignments
  • • Receive notifications
